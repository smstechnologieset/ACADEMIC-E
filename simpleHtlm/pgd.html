Admissions Criteria

Who Is Eligible to Apply?

Because Postgraduate Diplomas represent advanced graduate-level study, candidates should meet at least one of the qualifying pathways below.

1

Bachelor's Degree or Equivalent

Hold an accredited undergraduate degree (BA, BSc, or international equivalent) in any discipline from a recognized institution.

2

Professional Experience Pathway

Mid-career professionals with a Diploma or Advanced Diploma plus documented relevant workplace experience in management, IT, or public service.

3

National Identification (FAYDA ID / FIN)

Hold a valid Ethiopian National ID (Fayda / FIN) or passport for identity verification and digital certificate credentialing.

Got Questions?

Postgraduate Diploma FAQs

Everything you need to know about scheduling, coursework, examinations, and degree recognition.

Can I study while continuing full-time employment? ↓

Yes, absolutely. The Postgraduate Diploma programs are specifically architected for working professionals. Learning is 100% self-paced through the Skillsoft Percipio platform, accessible 24/7 on desktop and mobile. You can complete lectures, readings, and labs around your work schedule.

How are the six specialization electives selected? ↓

Upon admission, you will meet with your Academic Excellence academic advisor. You will select six courses from the elective menu that directly align with your industry, current role, or target promotion track. You can take them sequentially or concurrently as your schedule allows.

Who issues the diploma upon completion? ↓

Upon successful completion of all eight courses (six specialization + two soft skills) and passing the assessments, you will receive an internationally aligned Postgraduate Diploma certified in collaboration with University in New York, USA, along with cryptographically verifiable digital credentials.

How does the "My Business – My Strategies" simulation work? ↓

The simulation is a cloud-based executive sandbox where you operate a competitive enterprise. Over eight simulated fiscal quarters, you make real strategic decisions regarding product design, pricing, capital expenditure, and marketing. It connects theoretical principles with hands-on executive practice.

What if I fail an exam or coursework module? ↓

In accordance with Academic Excellence academic policies, students who do not achieve a passing mark on an initial attempt are eligible to repeat coursework or retake the assessment, subject to academic committee review and program scheduling.
