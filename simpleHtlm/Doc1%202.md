Academic Excellence

“The Pathway to Success”
Transforming Lives Through Global Education, Skills Development, and Workforce Readiness
Academic Excellence is a transformative educational initiative established to expand equitable access to internationally recognized education, workforce development, and career advancement opportunities for Ethiopian learners.

Operating through strategic partnerships with global educational institutions and philanthropic organizations, Academic Access serves as a bridge connecting Ethiopian students, graduates, professionals, entrepreneurs, public servants, and underserved communities with world-class learning opportunities designed to prepare them for success in the modern global economy.

The initiative is implemented in collaboration with the N, and RI, University in New York and other internationally recognized education providers.
Through a combination of advanced learning technologies, scholarship support, digital learning platforms, career services, and workforce development programs, Academic Access Ethiopia seeks to become one of the platforming delivering education access initiatives in Ethiopia.

Academic Excellence is dedicated to impowering professional development, skills training, and workforce readiness. Through innovative educational programs and international partnerships, Academic Excellence delivers transformative learning experiences that empower individuals and organizations to thrive in a rapidly evolving global economy.
Through strategic partnerships with leading organizations such as N, R, Skil and the University in New York part of one of the largest public university systems in the United States—we provide access to more than 10,000 specialized online courses across a wide range of disciplines, offering globally recognized learning programs designed to meet the demands of today's competitive job market. These programs cater to recent graduates, working professionals, government employees, and entrepreneurs, with a focus on:
Professional Skill Development
Reskilling and Upskilling Initiatives
Industry-Relevant Certifications
Productivity Enhancement Programs
Leadership and Career Advancement Training
By combining innovative learning methodologies with internationally benchmarked curricula, Academic Excellence equips learners with the knowledge and practical skills needed to succeed in the modern workplace.
At Academic Excellence with our partners N and E, we do not simply facilitate education—we facilitate possibilities, transform aspirations into opportunities, and invest in Ethiopia’s future.

Academic Excellence
At Academic Excellence, our mission is grounded in a strong and unwavering commitment to integrity, academic excellence, inclusivity, and social responsibility. At the heart of our work is a clear purpose: to facilitate access to quality, internationally recognized, and subsidized higher education opportunities for Ethiopians in partnership with N and R.
We believe that education is a fundamental driver of individual empowerment and national development. For many capable and ambitious Ethiopians, the cost and access of higher education can present a significant barrier to achieving their academic and professional aspirations. Academic Excellence is committed to helping overcome this barrier by creating pathways through which Ethiopian learners can access affordable, high-quality higher education and professional development opportunities in partnership with N and University in New York.
Our role extends beyond simply connecting learners with educational institutions. We seek to open doors to opportunity by facilitating subsidized education, supporting learners in identifying suitable academic pathways, and building partnerships that expand access to quality education. Through these efforts, we aim to ensure that financial circumstances do not unnecessarily prevent talented and motivated Ethiopians from pursuing higher education and reaching their full potential.
Every initiative, program, and partnership undertaken by Academic Excellence is guided by the principles of measurable impact. We carefully consider how our work can contribute to the educational, social, and economic advancement of Ethiopia. Our success is therefore measured not only by academic achievements, or professional advancement, but by the lasting positive difference our work creates in the lives of learners, their families, communities, and the organizations they serve.
Education as a Force for Transformation
By facilitating subsidized higher education, we aspire to help create a generation of knowledgeable professionals, responsible leaders, entrepreneurs, researchers, innovators, and socially conscious citizens. Job-Ready Global Learning Programs
International Learning Opportunities Through N, USA
The N, Washington, D.C., USA, is a globally recognized nonprofit organization committed to bridging employment, digital, academic, economic, and social divides through accessible, high-quality education. In partnership with the University in New York, N in partnership with Academic Excellence offers more than 120 Diploma and Certificate programs in high-demand fields, providing students and professionals with affordable, career-focused learning opportunities.
Academic Excellence is proud to bring these internationally recognized programs to Ethiopia, helping learners develop the skills needed to succeed in today's competitive and rapidly evolving workforce.
Learning Through Skillsoft Perception
All courses are delivered through Sk Perception, a leading online learning platform offering world-class content, interactive learning experiences, and practical skill development.
Program Areas
Students can choose from over 120 Diploma and Certificate programs in four key categories:
Business Skills
Productivity and Collaboration Tools
Information Technology (IT) Skills
Professional Certifications
Most programs can be completed within 24 weeks, offering flexibility for both students and working professionals.
What Are Job-Ready Courses?
Job-Ready Courses are structured learning pathways designed to align education with industry requirements and workforce demands. Drawing from a library of nearly 10,000 learning resources, these programs help learners acquire practical skills that lead to employment, promotions, career advancement, and higher earning potential.
Popular learning pathways include:
Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking
Cloud Computing
Machine Learning
Project Management

Professional Certifications
Why Job-Ready Skills Matter
As industries continue to evolve through technology and digital transformation, professionals must continuously update their knowledge and skills. Academic Excellence's programs are designed to help learners remain competitive, adapt to changing workplace demands, and prepare for future career opportunities.
Whether you are entering the workforce, seeking career advancement, or transitioning into a new field, our flexible online learning programs provide the tools and knowledge needed for success.
Internationally Recognized Certifications
Upon successful completion, learners receive certifications associated with the University in New York, USA. These globally recognized credentials validate professional competencies and enhance employment and career advancement opportunities worldwide.
Merit Badge Recognition
Students who achieve a GPA of 3.6 or higher (90% and above) are eligible for a Merit Badge recognizing outstanding academic performance. High-achieving learners may also receive professional recognition and testimonials that strengthen their career profiles and improve employment prospects.
