Curriculum Overview

What Are Job-Ready Courses?

Job-Ready Courses are structured learning pathways designed to align education with industry requirements and workforce demands. Drawing from a library of nearly 10,000 learning resources, these programs help learners acquire practical skills that lead to employment, promotions, career advancement, and higher earning potential. Most programs can be completed within 24 weeks, offering flexibility for both students and working professionals.

Students can choose from over 120 Diploma and Certificate programs in four key categories: Business Skills, Productivity and Collaboration Tools, Information Technology (IT) Skills, and Professional Certifications.

Internationally Recognized Certifications: Upon successful completion, learners receive certifications associated with the University in New York, USA. Students achieving a GPA of 3.6 or higher (90%+) are eligible for a Merit Badge recognizing outstanding academic performance.

Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) Programs

Through its partnership with the University in New York, USA, Academic Excellence offers internationally aligned Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) programs designed to equip professionals and graduates with advanced knowledge, practical skills, and globally relevant competencies.

Each PG Diploma program includes: Six specialization courses • Two professional soft-skill courses • 80+ hours of learning per specialization course • Flexible online learning over 12 months • Access to webinars, mentoring sessions, and expert-led discussions • Course delivery through the Skillsoft Percipio Learning Platform.

Students achieving a final score of 95% and above are awarded the prestigious Medallion of Merit, recognizing exceptional academic achievement.

Available PGD Programs:
  • 🎓 Postgraduate Diploma in Management
  • 💻 Postgraduate Diploma in Information Technology
  • 🧠 Postgraduate Diploma in Artificial Intelligence & Generative AI (AI/GAI)
