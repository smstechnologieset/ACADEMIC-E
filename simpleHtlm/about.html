Our Purpose & Commitment

Education as a Force for Transformation

Operating through strategic partnerships with global educational institutions and philanthropic organizations, Academic Excellence serves as a bridge connecting Ethiopian students, graduates, professionals, entrepreneurs, public servants, and underserved communities with world-class learning opportunities designed to prepare them for success in the modern global economy.

At Academic Excellence, our mission is grounded in a strong and unwavering commitment to integrity, academic rigor, inclusivity, and social responsibility. For many capable and ambitious Ethiopians, the cost and accessibility of higher education present a significant barrier. We are committed to helping overcome this barrier by creating pathways through which Ethiopian learners can access affordable, high-quality, and subsidized higher education.

Our role extends beyond simply connecting learners with educational institutions. We open doors by facilitating subsidized education, supporting learners in identifying suitable academic pathways, and building partnerships that ensure financial circumstances do not prevent talented and motivated individuals from reaching their full potential.

A Catalyst for National Advancement: Educated and empowered people strengthen families, build successful organizations, create employment, and participate effectively in the socio-economic development of Ethiopia.

Our Mission & Motto

Our Mission

Expanding Opportunities & Workforce Competencies

To expand access to affordable, internationally recognized education and workforce training opportunities that equip Ethiopian learners with the practical skills, technical competencies, and credentials needed to succeed in an increasingly digital and knowledge-driven world.

Our Motto

“Empowering People. Expanding Opportunity. Transforming Futures.”

Our motto reflects our unwavering dedication to empowering individuals and communities through continuous learning. By delivering globally relevant educational programs, we build the skilled human capital necessary for Ethiopia’s long-term economic prosperity.

Empowering Ethiopia's Future Workforce

Through subsidized learning programs, Ethiopian youth, professionals, and entrepreneurs gain direct access to specialized skill-based curricula developed by Skillsoft Percipio and certified by the University in New York. These initiatives help make world-class, career-focused education accessible and affordable, supporting Ethiopia's vision for sustainable development.

Our comprehensive curriculum focuses on five vital pillars:

  • 01
    Professional Skill Development: Essential workplace competencies, business communication, and critical thinking.
  • 02
    Reskilling and Upskilling Initiatives: Empowering working professionals to transition into modern digital roles.
  • 03
    Industry-Relevant Certifications: Preparation for globally respected vendor and university credentials.
  • 04
    Productivity Enhancement Programs: Modern software, project management tools, and collaboration suites.
  • 05
    Leadership and Career Advancement: Executive decision-making, strategic management, and entrepreneurial growth.

University in New York, USA

Accredited academic partner providing institutional oversight, syllabus validation, and internationally recognized certification upon successful graduation.

Skillsoft Percipio Learning Platform

Enterprise digital campus providing 24/7 access to over 10,000 video lectures, interactive coding sandboxes, audiobooks, and micro-learning modules.

Academic Honors & Merit Badges

Students achieving a GPA of 3.6+ (90%+) receive a Merit Badge, and graduates scoring 95%+ are awarded the prestigious Medallion of Merit.
